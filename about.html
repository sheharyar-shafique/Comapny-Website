THE STORY

From industrial machinery to intelligent technology.

Aozal Trading Company supplies machinery parts to factories, sourcing and importing industrial components from China and other countries to help businesses keep moving.

Inspired by that enterprise and the AOZAL name, Aozal Technologies begins a new chapter: bringing creativity, software engineering, AI services, and e-commerce expertise into one technology-focused company.

Different industries, connected by a belief in building useful things, solving real problems, and moving forward.

02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for tiktok shop restricted account reactivation—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Account status review

Collect the restriction notice, affected listings and current appeal eligibility.

02 / 08↗

Root-cause analysis

Identify relevant policy categories, previous warnings and operational gaps.

03 / 08↗

Evidence gathering

Organize genuine business, supplier, order and identity records requested by the platform.

04 / 08↗

Corrective actions

Fix eligible listing, fulfillment or documentation issues before appeal where possible.

05 / 08↗

Appeal preparation

Draft a factual explanation tied to the cited policy and verified evidence.

06 / 08↗

Official submission

Submit through the platform’s permitted appeal route without misrepresentation.

07 / 08↗

Follow-up management

Track case status and respond to permitted requests for more information.

08 / 08↗

Prevention plan

Implement compliance checks and operating changes to reduce recurring issues.

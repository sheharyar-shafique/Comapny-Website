02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for dropshipping—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Model and policy check

Confirm the destination marketplace permits the proposed fulfillment approach and product types.

02 / 08↗

Product validation

Research demand, competition, shipping constraints and realistic net margins.

03 / 08↗

Supplier due diligence

Check authenticity, inventory consistency, packing, tracking, quality and return terms.

04 / 08↗

Store and catalog setup

Create accurate listings, pricing rules and fulfillment expectations.

05 / 08↗

Order workflow

Document order processing, supplier communication, tracking and customer updates.

06 / 08↗

Exception handling

Manage delays, out-of-stock situations, returns and damaged parcels.

07 / 08↗

Compliance and quality

Review claims, IP rights, seller policy and performance indicators.

08 / 08↗

Optimization

Measure cancellations, delivery time, profit and product performance; refine assortment.

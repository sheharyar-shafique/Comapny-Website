02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for fbs brand building with private label—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Category and demand research

Study buyer needs, pricing, competition, regulations and potential margins.

02 / 08↗

Brand strategy

Define a distinctive name, positioning, packaging direction and customer promise.

03 / 08↗

Supplier checks

Compare samples, lead times, minimum orders, documentation and quality expectations.

04 / 08↗

Unit economics

Calculate landed cost, fees, returns allowance and practical inventory requirements.

05 / 08↗

Label and listing assets

Prepare packaging specifications, product photography and compliant claims.

06 / 08↗

FBS workflow setup

Plan stock ownership, packing, shipping, handling times and returns.

07 / 08↗

Launch and listing tests

Publish eligible listings, review search visibility and measure conversion.

08 / 08↗

Growth and operations

Adjust assortment, replenishment, pricing and customer experience using sales data.

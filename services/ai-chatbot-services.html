02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for ai chatbot services—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Use-case discovery

Identify audiences, support volume, required languages, answer sources and success measures.

02 / 08↗

Knowledge preparation

Collect approved FAQs, documents and records; clean, segment and version the content.

03 / 08↗

Conversation UX

Plan greetings, follow-up questions, forms, escalation and error recovery.

04 / 08↗

AI pipeline design

Choose prompting, retrieval, tools, memory boundaries and safe response rules.

05 / 08↗

Channel & API integration

Connect the website or messaging channel and permitted business systems.

06 / 08↗

Evaluation

Test answer accuracy, retrieval relevance, sensitive queries, injection resistance and edge cases.

07 / 08↗

Release

Deploy with access controls, feedback collection and human escalation.

08 / 08↗

Optimization

Review quality indicators, refresh knowledge and improve workflows over time.

02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for ai call bots services—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Call-use-case planning

Specify inbound or permitted outbound workflows, escalation policies, language support and KPIs.

02 / 08↗

Conversation design

Create intent maps, greetings, confirmations, structured data capture and human handoff rules.

03 / 08↗

Telephony connection

Configure phone routing, call webhooks, media streaming and graceful failover.

04 / 08↗

AI voice pipeline

Connect speech recognition, language model, tools and synthesized speech with latency targets.

05 / 08↗

Business integrations

Securely link appointments, CRM lookups, order status or internal knowledge as appropriate.

06 / 08↗

Safety & compliance

Handle consent, recording disclosure, sensitive data and applicable calling rules.

07 / 08↗

Call simulation & testing

Test interruptions, accents, noisy audio, wrong numbers, tool failures and escalation.

08 / 08↗

Launch & continuous tuning

Deploy, review transcripts where permitted, monitor latency and refine responses.

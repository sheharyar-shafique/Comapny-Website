02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for fine-tuning services—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Fit assessment

Decide whether fine-tuning is appropriate compared with prompting, retrieval or rules.

02 / 08↗

Data collection

Gather permitted training samples and define quality, privacy and consent requirements.

03 / 08↗

Cleaning & formatting

Deduplicate, normalize and label examples; split training, validation and test sets.

04 / 08↗

Baseline evaluation

Record the current model performance on a stable, representative test suite.

05 / 08↗

Training

Select an allowed technique and model, run experiments and track configurations.

06 / 08↗

Post-training evaluation

Measure improvement, regression, safety, overfitting and real-world behavior.

07 / 08↗

Deployment & rollback

Version adapters or model artifacts, integrate inference and maintain a rollback path.

08 / 08↗

Monitoring

Track production drift and retrain only when the evidence supports it.

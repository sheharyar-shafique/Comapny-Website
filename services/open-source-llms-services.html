02 / END-TO-END DELIVERY

How it works.

The complete workflow for open-source llms services—from discovery and planning through implementation, launch and ongoing care.

01 / 08↗

Requirements and constraints

Clarify privacy, latency, context size, languages, GPU budget and hosting preferences.

02 / 08↗

Model/license review

Evaluate candidate models, their usage terms, performance and hardware footprint.

03 / 08↗

Infrastructure planning

Provision GPU or CPU inference environments with secure networking and storage.

04 / 08↗

Model serving

Configure runtime, quantization, batching and authenticated inference endpoints.

05 / 08↗

Application integration

Add retrieval, tools, logging and business workflows as needed.

06 / 08↗

Benchmark & safety testing

Evaluate accuracy, throughput, hallucinations, privacy and adversarial inputs.

07 / 08↗

Go-live

Deploy with scaling, monitoring, rollback and versioning.

08 / 08↗

Ongoing optimization

Tune inference costs, update models and re-run benchmarks.
